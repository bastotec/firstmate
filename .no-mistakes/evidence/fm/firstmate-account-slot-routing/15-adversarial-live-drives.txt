Adversarial drives against the live four-slot home
==================================================

Same real home and real quota-axi producer as 14-live-four-slot-probe-after-fixes.txt.
Each case is a configuration an operator could really have; the question is
whether routing fails closed and says why without naming an account.

--- a slot rebound to a DIFFERENT account identity than its store signs in as ---
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe-all claude-a claude-b
  claude-a	available
  claude-b	UNAVAILABLE  slot 'claude-b' returned stale, mismatched, or malformed quota evidence
The healthy sibling still routes. The refusal names the logical slot only.

--- a slot store with no vendor credential at all ---
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe-all codex-a codex-b
  codex-a	available
  codex-b	UNAVAILABLE  slot 'codex-b' is unavailable: its store holds no vendor-managed credential

--- ambient credentials exported by the caller must not serve any slot ---
$ ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=... OPENAI_API_KEY=... CODEX_API_KEY=... \
  CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=/nonexistent CODEX_HOME=/nonexistent \
  fm-account-slot.sh probe-all claude-a codex-a
  claude-a	available
  codex-a	available
what the producer actually received:
  PROBE argv=[--provider claude --profile-only --full --json --no-credential-refresh] profile-only=yes CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=<LAB>/profiles/claude-a CODEX_HOME=<unset> ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=<unset> OPENAI_API_KEY=<unset> CODEX_API_KEY=<unset> CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN=<unset>
  PROBE argv=[--provider codex --profile-only --full --json --no-credential-refresh] profile-only=yes CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR=<unset> CODEX_HOME=<LAB>/profiles/codex-a ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=<unset> OPENAI_API_KEY=<unset> CODEX_API_KEY=<unset> CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN=<unset>
The caller's ambient keys and store overrides were stripped; each probe saw only
its own slot store.

--- refusals ---
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe-all nope-a
  -> error: slot 'nope-a' is not configured in this home   (exit 1)
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe-all default
  -> error: slot 'default' is not configured in this home   (exit 1)
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe default
  -> error: account slot 'default' is reserved for clearing an account selection   (exit 1)
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe claude-a claude-b
  -> usage: fm-account-slot.sh probe <slot>   (exit 2)
$ fm-account-slot.sh probe-all
  -> usage: fm-account-slot.sh probe-all <slot>...   (exit 2)

--- dispatch rules that reference slots wrongly ---
$ a claude profile pointed at a codex slot
  -> error: slot reference is missing or belongs to another harness: codex-a
$ the same slot listed twice for one effective tuple
  -> error: config/crew-dispatch.json is invalid - accountSlots entries must be unique
