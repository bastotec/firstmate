The repo's own opt-in live check, and the one thing this host cannot provide
===========================================================================

tests/fm-account-slot-live-e2e.test.sh is the change's own end-to-end check
against real subscriptions.

--- default: the gate is closed ---
$ bash tests/fm-account-slot-live-e2e.test.sh
skip: live: opt-in; set FM_ACCOUNT_SLOT_LIVE_E2E=1 to run
(exit 0)

--- opted in, against the provisioned four-slot lab home ---
$ FM_ACCOUNT_SLOT_LIVE_E2E=1 FM_HOME=<lab> bash tests/fm-account-slot-live-e2e.test.sh
not ok - each provider's two slots must name distinct expected identities (expected '2	2', got '1	1')
(exit 1)

This is the check doing its job, not a product failure. It requires two DIFFERENT
Claude subscriptions and two DIFFERENT Codex subscriptions. This host holds one
of each, so the lab's claude-a/claude-b and codex-a/codex-b are two isolated
stores per provider signed in to the same account, and the check correctly
refuses to call that a two-subscription verification.

Everything the check would otherwise assert - four isolated profile-only probes,
two per provider, four fresh sanitized provider rows, and no account or
provenance field in the output - was driven directly against the same real
producer in 14-live-four-slot-probe-after-fixes.txt.

To close this gap: sign a second Claude subscription into a second
CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR and a second Codex subscription into a second CODEX_HOME,
point claude-b and codex-b at those stores with their own expectedAccountId, and
re-run the command above.
