The stray double quotes in the published charter are not cosmetic. They come
from bash 3.2's pattern substitution, which takes the quotes around the
replacement literally when the expansion sits inside a double-quoted word.

bin/fm-remote-home-seed.sh:160-161

  line=${line//"$PARENT_STATUS"/"$REMOTE_STATUS"}        <- assignment, clean
  printf '%s\n' "${line//"$PARENT_INBOX"/"$REMOTE_INBOX"}"  <- quoted word, adds "

That is why the status path now renders cleanly and the inbox path does not:
the change moved the status rewrite into the assignment and left the new inbox
rewrite in the position that carries the bug.

GNU bash, version 3.2.57(1)-release (arm64-apple-darwin25)
assignment form : in '/remote/y.inbox'.
inside a quote  : in '"/remote/y.inbox"'.
